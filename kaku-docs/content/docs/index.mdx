# What is Kaku and why should I use it?

Kaku is a handwriting app for all platforms (iOS/iPadOS, Android, Windows, Mac, Linux, Web) that is entirely open source and [free as in freedom, not free beer](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/free-sw.en.html), licensed under the [GNU AGPL v3](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html). It is a fork of the great handwriting app called [Write by styluslabs](https://www.styluslabs.com/), modernized. See my [AI code disclosure](./ai-code-disclosure.mdx).&#x20;

It has most features of a modern handwriting app, and some innovative ones other apps don't have (many from Write). Including, but not limited to:

- [Notebook organization with tags (done right!)](./notebook-organization.mdx)
- [Shape recognition](./the-tools/shape-snapping.mdx)
- [All handwriting tools at your reach](./the-tools/index.mdx)
- Modern UI
- Outlines and layers
- Ruled pages done right <Chip color="green">innovation</Chip>
  - Kaku knows your paper layout, so it can e.g. move things one line down or reflow your text.
- Page scanning <Chip color="red">untested!</Chip>
- [Undo and redo done right](./learn-the-basics.mdx#undo-and-redo) <Chip color="green">innovation</Chip>

and so, *so* much more. You can tell I'm excited to tell you about all this. Nevertheless I try to keep my pages short and digestable so you don't have to fight through pages upon pages of explanations just to use Kaku.
