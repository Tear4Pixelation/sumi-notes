## Shape Snapping

When you keep holding your [pen](./pens.mdx) in one spot for a set amount of time (set in settings), Kaku will try to snap that stroke to a shape. It currently supports:

- Lines
- Ellipses
- Rectangles
- Scribbles: Draw a zigzag line over something you wrote and long press to erase it.
