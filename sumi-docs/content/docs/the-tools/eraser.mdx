The eraser allows you to remove parts of strokes or entire strokes using an eraser of a certain thickness.

The two options are:

## Stroke erase

The first toggle controls whether the eraser erases the whole stroke or just parts of it.

## Lined erase

When toggled, the eraser will only erase strokes that belong to this line. Useful when your handwriting from the line above overlaps with your current line.

And after that we have the [the switch back toggle](./index.mdx#switchback-toggle).
